# Impeccable

## Design Context

### Users

Builders of AI agents — mostly technical, working inside a multi-tenant SaaS with
organizations, projects, credentials and billing. They spend long sessions in the
product: composing workflows on a React Flow canvas, configuring agents, reading
execution traces. Sessions are deliberate and lengthy, not glance-and-go.

For Voice Agents specifically the job is: configure a conversational agent, then
immediately hear whether it sounds right. The loop is *tune → listen → tune*, so the
call is not a side feature — it is the feedback mechanism the whole page exists to
serve.

### Brand Personality

**Calm studio** — confident, spacious, expensive. Building agents should feel like
creative work, not like filling in a configuration form. Closer to ElevenLabs and
Anthropic than to Linear or Vercel: the product is not trying to look maximally
dense or maximally technical, it is trying to feel considered.

Three words: **assured, unhurried, precise.**

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Theme:** light is the primary theme and gets the polish; dark must stay fully
  functional and correct, but light is what is designed against.
- **Reference:** ElevenLabs Agents. Two things specifically — the **living call
  visual** (the voice element is the hero of the screen, not a button in a corner)
  and the **density and typography** (calm cards, generous air, careful text
  hierarchy, soft borders).
- **Anti-reference:** a settings page. Stacked label-over-input rows with uniform
  spacing, a heading and a paragraph restating each other, a lone full-width button.
  That is what "cheap" looked like here.
- **Existing system:** Tailwind 4 + shadcn/ui (new-york), indigo `#6366f1` accent,
  `--radius: 0.5rem`. Work inside these tokens — the product already has a visual
  language and this section should join it, not fork it.

### Design Principles

1. **The call is the subject.** On an agent's page the live call gets the space,
   the motion and the attention. Configuration supports it.
2. **Motion must be earned by data.** Anything that moves should move because real
   audio, real state or real latency changed — never as decoration.
3. **Density through grouping, not compression.** Related fields sit together on one
   line; unrelated groups get real separation. Do not shrink everything uniformly.
4. **Show the machine honestly.** Latency, who is speaking, what was heard — surface
   the real numbers and state rather than a reassuring animation.
5. **Every state is designed.** Idle, connecting, live, interrupted, failed and empty
   each get deliberate treatment; none may look like an afterthought.
